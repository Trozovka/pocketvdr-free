PocketVDR Free
A personal voyage data recorder on your Android phone. It records position, time, speed and heading for the whole voyage using only the phone's GPS. No internet, no account.

Mark incidents with one button, review your track, and export it as GPX or NMEA (for OpenCPN) or as a plain text log for an insurer or surveyor.

Free: recording is unlimited forever. Review and export of all history for the first 30 days, then the last 24 hours or the current voyage. PocketVDR Pro keeps all history.

Not a type-approved VDR or S-VDR under SOLAS and not legal evidence. A personal record only.

Installing
Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Coming soon to Google Play; until then you install the APK file. Android will show an "unrecognized app" / Play Protect warning and ask you to allow installs from this source the first time. That is normal for apps installed outside Google Play.
